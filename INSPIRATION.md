# Inspiration

Personal sites looked at while this one was rebuilt, and the one company site named with them. The home page lists the same sites, from `src/data/inspiration.ts`. `DESIGN.md` is what the redesign kept. This file is why each one is here.

Add an entry when a new site is used. Give the address, who it is, what was taken from it, and whether the page changed.

## In the current page

### [Michael Cohen](https://www.michaelcohen.io/)

One page: a short introduction, a dated work history, writing and talks. The November 2026 redesign started from the instruction to make this site look almost exactly like that page, and to keep it as the overall visual direction while bringing back themes from the previous kylecesmat.com. The layout, design tokens and component structure are adapted from the MIT-licensed source. The credit and the licence are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

### [Toni Gemayel](https://tonigemayel.com/about)

A personal site. The about page is a short self-portrait: where he is, what he is into, and a list of people. It was one of four references in the September 2026 rewrite. A later pass in that rewrite leaned on the quieter pages below. The November redesign asked for this about page again, and `DESIGN.md` records it as a reference for the page that shipped.

### [The Clearing Company](https://www.clearing.co/)

A company site. Toni Gemayel is listed there as CEO. It was named in the same November note as his about page, and `DESIGN.md` records it as the other reference for the page that shipped.

## Looked at during the September rewrite

These three were the other references in that pass. The November redesign took its layout from michaelcohen.io, and took Toni Gemayel's about page and clearing.co as its extra references.

### [Stefano J. Attardi](https://attardi.org/)

A personal site: name and title, writing, and a short list of projects. The September pass settled on this page, with nickbytes.com, for a quiet editorial feel. Sparse navigation, weight on the writing, a clean title, and proof shown with restraint.

### [Nick Beattie](https://nickbytes.com/)

A personal site. Engineering at Coinbase. The homepage is a name, a line of history, and a long list of links. The September pass used it for the same quiet weight as attardi.org, and noted the link list as a possible "things I like" pattern. The home page now lists these sites by name.

### [Nick Gavalas](https://ngavalas.com/)

A personal site written as one long lowercase letter about developer infrastructure. It was in the September set of four. The pass that followed kept the quieter pages.
